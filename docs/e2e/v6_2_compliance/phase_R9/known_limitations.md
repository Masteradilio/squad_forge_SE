# Phase R9 Known Limitations

This is candidate evidence, not final release acceptance.

Open limitations:

- The observed corpus is a controlled compliance fixture, not a statistically
  significant production benchmark across real customer repositories.
- JSONL publication/export is not yet generated from the same aggregate data.
- CI row-count validation for generated publications is still open.
- Deep Swarm remains opt-in and `PARTIAL` until repeated controlled executions
  show safe superiority.
- The corpus captures fixture licenses and provenance but does not yet include
  a separate license document per fixture directory.
